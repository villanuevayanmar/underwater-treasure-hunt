# Storyline — The Lost Treasure

A long time ago, a valuable treasure chest was hidden inside a cave deep beneath the ocean. Over time, the cave became difficult to find. Rocks and narrow paths now make the area dangerous for explorers.

The player becomes the pilot of a small yellow submarine and begins an underwater expedition. The submarine travels through the ocean while fish swim nearby and bubbles rise toward the surface.

The pilot must explore carefully, avoid the large rocks, and find the dark cave on the right side of the ocean. When the submarine gets close to the treasure chest, the pilot can press **SPACE** or tap **COLLECT**.

Once the treasure is collected, the mission ends. The game displays the exploration time and attempts to save the mission result in `mission_result.txt`.

The player can then start another expedition or return to the title screen.
